# Kungfu Community Code of Conduct

Kungfu welcomes technical criticism, bug reports, questions, and disagreement.
Participation must remain safe enough for people to report failures honestly.

## Expected conduct

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not a person's worth or motives.
- Be specific, patient, and respectful across language and experience levels.
- Protect private data. Never pressure someone to publish credentials, customer
  material, private logs, or vulnerability details.
- Correct mistakes openly and preserve technical context that helps resolution.
- Respect a maintainer's request to move a vulnerability to private reporting
  or to stop abusive contact.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, impersonation,
coordinated disruption, spam, and deliberate publication of secrets or
exploitable vulnerability details are not accepted.

Strong criticism, inconvenient bug reports, and negative product feedback are
not abuse. Moderation must never be used to hide them.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary public conduct problems, use GitHub's report and block controls and
preserve the relevant URLs, timestamps, and screenshots. Do not repost harmful
material merely to create evidence.

If a report contains a vulnerability, credential, private user data, or an
exploitable bypass, use
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/kungfu-systems/kungfu/security/advisories/new).
Do not open a public issue or Discussion.

The launch moderation operator records the evidence and proposes a reversible
response. The escalation owner makes consequential decisions, including content
removal, account blocking, or temporary interaction limits. Responses should be
proportionate, documented, and limited to the abusive behavior. Affected
participants may ask for reconsideration through a new, non-abusive GitHub
thread or private security report when the evidence itself is sensitive.

During Alpha launch operations, the exact roles, thresholds, evidence rules, and
interaction-limit rollback procedure are defined in the
[Alpha attention operations runbook](docs/development/alpha-attention-operations.md).
