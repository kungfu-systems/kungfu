## Context

Kungfu already has two adjacent capabilities:

- KFD-1 declares the load-bearing contract world whose facts must not drift;
- the runtime ledger records user and product facts as replayable Episodes.

Without an explicit bridge, KFD can remain a release-governance system while
the ledger accepts domain data without saying what the data means, who owns its
schema, whether it belongs to the declared world, or how a trust claim binds to
it. Conversely, treating every recorded event as automatically KFD-1 compliant
would confuse durable observation with external truth.

The missing contract is the path by which a product or user declares a fact
surface, admits observations into it, preserves their history, queries them
under the declaration effective at that cut, and asks KFD-2 to assess a claim.

## Decision

Kungfu will make KFD-1 contract-world declarations a first-class coordinate of
runtime fact admission and querying:

```text
KFD-1 declaration
  -> fact-surface admission
  -> Episode history and sealed roots
  -> QueryBasis at a declared cut
  -> KFD-2 trust assessment
```

These stages have separate responsibilities:

- **KFD-1 declares the fact world.** It defines what may count as a fact for a
  load-bearing contract world, including schema owner, source authority,
  identity, compatibility, and interpretation rules.
- **Episode preserves fact history.** It records observations, admissions,
  corrections, retractions, and causal evolution without rewriting the past.
- **KFD-2 assesses trust.** It binds a concrete claim to admitted facts,
  evidence, derivation, responsibility, and residual risk.

No stage substitutes for another.

### 1. Product facts and user facts use the same ontology path

Kungfu's own KFD-1 declarations define core product contract worlds. A user or
extension may declare a domain contract world and one or more fact surfaces
inside it. The core does not need to understand trading orders, agent prompts,
game entities, medical cases, or another domain's vocabulary.

A `ContractWorldDeclaration` has stable identity and version, an effective
system-time range, and references to its registered contract surfaces. A
`FactSurfaceDeclaration` identifies at least:

- contract-world id and declaration version or content root;
- fact-surface id and its single schema owner under KF-ADR-019f86da-4f90-71eb-b4c0-376ca7bc7ad3;
- admitted source identities or source-authority policy;
- identity/key and valid-time semantics;
- reducer, correction, retraction, conflict, and redaction rules;
- compatibility classification and known limits.

The declaration may be authored outside the journal, but the runtime binds the
exact registered declaration hash/root into admission and proof. Generated
JSON, SQLite tables, GUI state, and SDK code are projections, not additional
declaration authorities.

### 2. Recording is not automatic admission or external truth

An observation may be durably recorded before Kungfu can admit it to a declared
fact surface. Admission has explicit, inspectable outcomes:

```text
admitted
unregistered-surface
incompatible-schema
ambiguous-authority
unverifiable
```

Unknown or rejected observations remain available for diagnosis and later
repair. They are not silently dropped, silently coerced, or included in a
canonical fold.

`admitted` means that the observation satisfied the declaration and integrity
rules effective at that system-time cut. It does not prove that the observation
is a universally true statement about the external world. Source claims may
conflict; the canonical fact may be that two admitted sources disagree.

### 3. Admission is journaled and replayable

Admission, correction, retraction, and declaration change are facts in the
runtime ledger. Their records bind the observation or accepted range to the
contract-world declaration and fact-surface declaration that governed it.

Replay must reproduce the same admission outcome when the same authoritative
records, declarations, schemas, and policies are available. Sealed Episode
roots commit the relevant history; a mutable registry lookup at replay time
must not reinterpret an older Episode under today's declaration.

A declaration version may supersede another prospectively. Historical cuts use
the declaration effective at that cut. A migration or reclassification is a
new, explicit fact with its own evidence, not a retroactive edit.

Protected delivery evidence follows the same temporal discipline. For a GitHub
Merge Queue delivery, the synthetic merge-group validation run completes
before GitHub advances that exact commit onto the protected branch. Native
admission therefore requires:

```text
merge-group run completion <= protected merge <= observation
```

Freshness and admission lag start at the protected merge. Reversing the first
two events would either reject every real queue delivery or misdescribe a
pre-merge validation run as a post-merge action.

### 4. QueryBasis pins the contract world

KF-ADR-019f86da-4f90-7e38-b72f-ef8829e14104's basis becomes:

```text
FactState(contract_world, scope, perspective, cut, policy)
  = fold(records admitted under the pinned declarations)
```

The basis and proof envelope identify the contract-world declaration and every
fact-surface declaration used by the fold. A query that omits this coordinate
may inspect raw observations, but it cannot describe its result as canonical
fact state for that contract world.

If declaration evidence is missing, incompatible, ambiguous, or outside its
effective range, the query reports that condition. It must not select the
newest local declaration as a convenience fallback.

### 5. KFD-2 trust reports bind claims to the full path

A KFD-2 assessment is not a property stamped onto rows. A `TrustReport` binds:

- the assessed claim and its intended use;
- contract-world and fact-surface declaration roots;
- QueryDefinition, cut, result, and lineage hashes where a query derives it;
- Episode ids, sealed roots, sources, accepted ranges, and admission outcomes;
- missing, conflicting, redacted, corrupt, or unverifiable evidence;
- responsibility, validation, residual risk, and assessment policy/version.

The report may conclude trusted-for-purpose, insufficient-evidence, conflict,
or another versioned assessment outcome. It must not turn KFD-1 conformance or
ledger durability into a claim of universal truth.

### 6. One typed semantic root serves every surface

The declaration, admission, query-basis, and trust-report contracts live in the
domain-neutral core. C++, Python, Node/TypeScript, Rust, CLI, GUI/TUI, and KFX
surfaces expose thin forms of the same objects.

The SDK may scaffold a fact surface; the runtime must validate and bind it. A
GUI may help author or inspect declarations; it cannot own them. SQL may query
admitted facts; it cannot bypass admission or change the contract world through
connection-local state.

KF-ADR-019f86da-4f90-71eb-b4c0-376ca7bc7ad3 still decides the persisted schema substrate. This ADR does not create
a third schema system: open/domain declarations and facts normally use one
FlatBuffers owner; compact closed-kernel admission metadata may use Hana POD
only when its kernel role is explicit and no duplicate owner is introduced.

## Implementation sequence

1. Define typed contract-world and fact-surface declaration references plus
   the admission-outcome vocabulary. Extend KF-ADR-019f86da-4f90-7e38-b72f-ef8829e14104 `QueryBasis` and lineage
   with declaration roots without changing Q0 exact-cut behavior.
2. Add append/fold/readback fixtures for admitted and non-admitted observations,
   including a historical declaration change and replay.
3. Make authority-scan and projection queries enforce the same admission rules;
   expose declaration and admission diagnostics through stable CLI/SDK output.
4. Define the first KFD-2 `TrustReport` over one proof-carrying query and expose
   it to agent and human reference surfaces.
5. Connect `kungfu sdk add fact-surface` scaffolding and Buildchain evidence to
   the same runtime declarations instead of a parallel registry semantics.

## Acceptance gates

- The same observation and pinned declaration reproduce the same admission
  outcome and historical query result.
- A declaration update cannot retroactively reinterpret an older cut.
- Unregistered, incompatible, ambiguous, and unverifiable observations remain
  inspectable but cannot enter the canonical fold.
- Query proof identifies the exact contract-world and fact-surface declarations
  used, including missing declaration evidence.
- One KFD-2 report binds a real claim to declarations, Episode/query proof,
  responsibility, and residual risk.
- C++, Python, Node/TypeScript, Rust, CLI, and GUI-facing fixtures do not invent
  independent admission meanings.
- Removing the GUI, Buildchain, or any optional projection leaves local
  declaration, admission, replay, query, and proof semantics intact.

## Consequences

- KFD becomes part of Kungfu's data semantics rather than only its release
  process.
- User-defined facts gain a governed route into the truth source without
  forcing domain vocabulary into the kernel.
- Historical queries remain interpretable after schemas and declarations
  evolve.
- Trust claims become reproducible objects over ledger evidence instead of
  prose attached to a result.
- Admission and trust add policy/versioning work; the implementation must start
  with one thin fact surface rather than a universal ontology engine.

## Alternatives considered

- **Treat every Episode record as KFD-1 compliant.** Rejected because durable
  capture does not establish schema ownership, authority, interpretation, or
  external truth.
- **Keep KFD only in Buildchain release evidence.** Rejected because product
  contracts and runtime facts would remain separate trust systems.
- **Resolve declarations only from the latest registry.** Rejected because
  replay and historical cuts would change when the registry changes.
- **Make trust a boolean column on each fact.** Rejected because trust is a
  claim- and purpose-specific assessment with evidence and residual risk.
- **Let each domain implement admission in its adapter.** Rejected because
  queries, replay, and proof would then have no shared semantic root.

## Residual risk

- Contract-world vocabulary can grow into a generic ontology platform; only
  fields required by executable admission, replay, query, or proof belong in
  the core.
- Source authority and external truth remain domain problems. Kungfu can prove
  what was declared, observed, admitted, and derived, not every real-world
  proposition.
- Migration across declaration versions requires explicit policy and evidence;
  this ADR forbids retroactive reinterpretation but does not prescribe every
  domain migration strategy.
